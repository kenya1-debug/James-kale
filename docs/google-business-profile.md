# Google Business Profile and local search: James Kale & Co. Advocates

Goal: appear when people in Kabarnet and Baringo search for "advocate near me", "land lawyer Kabarnet" and similar.

## 1. Claim or create the profile
1. Go to business.google.com and search for the firm. Claim it if it exists, or create it if not.
2. **Name:** exactly as on the website, "James Kale & Co. Advocates". Do not add keywords such as "Best Lawyer Kabarnet": Google removes such profiles.
3. **Category:** choose the closest primary category available (for example "Law firm" or "Lawyer"). Add secondary categories that match the real practice areas (family law, criminal law, real estate law, business law, employment law), choosing the closest ones Google offers.
4. **Address:** Lelbatai Building, 2nd Floor, Room 4, Kabarnet Town, Baringo County. Place the map pin exactly (0.4929, 35.7429). Add a landmark in the description, for example the nearby Aga Khan Hospital or Kabarnet Posta Office.
5. **Service area:** Kabarnet, Baringo County and the wider Rift Valley.
6. **Phone and website:** use the same number as the website. Website link: your site address followed by `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp` so visits from the profile show up in analytics.
7. **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, closed Saturday and Sunday. These must match the website and the Availability settings.

## 2. Verify
Google will ask for verification (postcard, phone, email or video). For an office on an upper floor, a video showing the building entrance, the signage and the office door is often required. Have it ready.

## 3. Complete the profile
- **Description (up to 750 characters):** plain, factual, no superlatives or guarantees. Mention the practice areas, the location and that consultations are by appointment.
- **Photos:** the building entrance, the office interior, the advocate (with his consent). No stock images. Add 5 to 10.
- **Services:** list each practice area using the same names as the website.
- **Appointment link:** point to the Book a Consultation page.
- **Posts:** share each new Insights article here. A short post every two weeks is enough.

## 4. Reviews: do this carefully
- Never pay for reviews, offer rewards, or write them yourself: Google penalises this, and it may breach advocate advertising rules.
- Before asking clients for reviews, confirm with the Law Society of Kenya rules and remember client confidentiality: clients decide what to share, and the firm should never confirm that someone is a client in a reply.
- Reply politely to every review without revealing any matter details.

## 5. Consistency (important for local ranking)
Use exactly the same name, address and phone number everywhere: the website footer, the Contact page, Google Business Profile, Facebook, LinkedIn, and any directory (for example the Law Society of Kenya directory). Small differences confuse search engines.

## 6. Google Search Console (free)
1. Go to search.google.com/search-console and add the live domain as a property.
2. Verify with the DNS record (Vercel can add it for you).
3. Submit `https://YOUR-DOMAIN/sitemap.xml`.
4. After launch, use URL Inspection on the home page, a practice area page and an article, and click Request Indexing.
5. Check back weekly at first: Pages (indexing problems) and Performance (what people searched).

## 7. After launch
- Publish one useful Insights article a month on questions clients really ask: land checks, succession, tenancy, debt recovery.
- Link new articles from the relevant practice area page.
- Keep the Availability and office hours in the dashboard up to date, including public holidays.
