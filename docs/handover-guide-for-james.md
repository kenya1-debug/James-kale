## 1. What the website does for you

- Visitors can read about you and your services in English and Kiswahili.
- They can **book a consultation** by choosing a day and time that you are free. You get an email, and the request appears in your admin page.
- They can **send you a message** on the Contact page.
- They can read your **articles** and call or WhatsApp you in one tap.
- Google can find and show your office on the map.

## 2. Signing in

1. Go to your website address and add **/admin** at the end.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Use **Sign out** (top right) when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

Keep your password private and unique. Do not use it on any other website.

## 3. Every day (2 minutes)

1. Open **Dashboard**. The numbers show new requests and new messages.
2. Open **Consultations**. Click a new request. You can call or WhatsApp the person from there.
3. After you contact them, set the status:
   - **New**: nobody has replied yet
   - **Contacted**: you have spoken to them
   - **Scheduled**: the appointment is agreed
   - **Closed**: finished or cancelled
4. Write private notes in the notes box. Only you and your staff can see them.
5. Do the same for **Messages**.

Tip: reply to new requests quickly. The website tells people you will contact them within one working day.

## 4. Changing your details (Site settings)

Open **Site settings** to change:
- your phone number, WhatsApp number and email (the Call and WhatsApp buttons use them)
- your biography, qualifications, year you were admitted, and your photo
- the Law Society or other memberships, and the courts you appear in
- your office hours text
- whether testimonials show on the home page

Click **Save** in each box. The website updates straight away.

## 5. Opening hours and days off (Availability)

- Tick the days you work and set opening, closing and lunch times. Clients can only book inside these times.
- **Appointment** is how long each booking lasts.
- Under **Days off**, add public holidays, court days or leave. People cannot book those dates.

## 6. Writing an article (Blog posts)

1. **Blog posts → New article**.
2. Give it a title and a short summary (this shows in Google).
3. Write in the big box. The buttons above it make headings, lists, links and bold text. The picture button adds a photo.
4. Choose **Status**:
   - **Draft**: only you can see it
   - **Published**: everyone can see it now
   - **Scheduled**: it appears by itself at the time you choose
5. Click **Save article**.

Good articles answer questions clients really ask: how to check land before buying, how succession works, tenant rights. Aim for one a month.
Please keep articles general. Do not write about a real client's matter.

## 7. Practice areas, FAQs and testimonials

- **Practice areas**: edit the text of each service page.
- **FAQs**: questions and answers shown on the home page and service pages.
- **Testimonials**: only add one if the client has agreed in writing, and check it complies with the Law Society of Kenya rules on advertising. Tick **Published** to show it, and turn on testimonials in Site settings.

## 8. Your staff (Team)

You can let a secretary handle bookings without giving access to everything.
- **Staff** can see Consultations and Messages only.
- **Admin** can change everything.

To add someone: ask whoever manages your website to create their login, then open **Team** and tick **Active**. When someone leaves, untick **Active** the same day.

## 9. Protecting client information

- The website stores names, phone numbers and the short description people write. Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you are responsible for this information.
- Only the people you activate can see it. Never share your login.
- If a person asks you to delete their information, open the request and use **Delete**.
- Never ask clients to send confidential documents through the website forms. Agree a safe way to share them.
- Use the **Export CSV** button to keep a copy, and store the file somewhere safe. Delete old copies you no longer need.

## 10. If something looks wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| You cannot sign in | Check the email and password. If still stuck, contact your website manager. |
| Booking emails stopped | Check the Consultations page: the requests still arrive there. Then tell your website manager. |
| A page looks wrong or has a typo | Fix it in the admin, or tell your website manager. |
| Someone says they could not book | Check Availability: are the days and times set? Is that date blocked? |

## 11. What the website costs to run

Your website manager will give you a list of the monthly and yearly costs (hosting, database, domain name). Keep this page with your records.
